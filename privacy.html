Privacy Policy

Ryo Games · Effective date: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play games made by Ryo Games, including Stacker and Animal Merge (Android). Ryo Games is an independent developer. If you have any questions, contact us at ryostu.game@gmail.com.

Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email or any other personal details. There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your progress, best score and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The games show optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to serve and measure those ads, as described below.
  • Animal Merge uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played (for example how long a run lasts). This data is tied to a random ID, not to your name, and is not used for advertising.

Information stored on your device

Each game keeps a small save file on your device containing your progress (for example your best score, the current run and the animals you have discovered) and your sound, music and vibration settings (where the game has them). It never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our games use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to offer optional rewarded video ads (for example "Watch Ad" to continue a run, or to double a bonus). An ad is only shown when you choose to tap that button. To have an ad ready, the SDK starts when the game opens and loads ads in the background.

Google, as the ad provider, may collect and process the following to deliver, measure and protect ads (including fraud prevention):

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app-set ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps, video completion) and diagnostics.

This means the data is shared with Google and, where you consent, with the advertising partners listed in the consent message. It is handled under Google's own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Ryo Games does not receive this data in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were shown).

Game analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase, Animal Merge only)

Animal Merge uses Google Analytics for Firebase so we can see how the game is played and make it better: which screens players use, how long runs last, where players stop, and which buttons they tap. Stacker does not use it.

What is collected:

  • a random ID for this installation of the app (the app-instance ID). It is not your name, email or phone number, and a new one is made if you reinstall the game or clear its data;
  • game events, for example: a run started or ended, its score, length and number of drops, screens viewed, buttons tapped, whether a rewarded ad was offered and watched, and animals discovered;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version) and approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address;
  • automatic usage measures such as first open, sessions and time spent in the app.

We have turned off the advertising features of Google Analytics: this data is not used to show you ads, not used for ad personalisation, and not linked to your advertising ID. Google processes it on our behalf under the Firebase privacy and security information, the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We see it only in Google's reports, grouped and without names.

Where consent is required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), analytics runs only if you choose Consent, or allow "Store and/or access information on a device" under Manage options. If you refuse, or the consent message cannot load, no analytics data is sent. In other regions, game analytics is on by default and there is no in-game switch; uninstalling the game stops it.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): when you first open the game, and again if your choices expire or our partners change, the game shows Google's consent message, where you can consent, refuse, or manage your choices. If you refuse, any ads shown will not be personalised, and sometimes no ad will be available. In Animal Merge the same choice also turns game analytics on or off.
  • Change your choice later: in Animal Merge, open Settings from the title screen and tap Privacy options. In Stacker, tap the gear icon and then Privacy options. The button appears only in regions where it is required.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Not watching ads: ads in our games are optional; you can always decline the offer and keep playing.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our games, contact us and we will explain how to stop collection and reset the game's data.

Data retention, deletion and security

Data on your device stays there until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Google keeps event-level analytics data (Animal Merge) linked to the app-instance ID for up to 14 months, then deletes it automatically. Aggregated statistics that do not identify any device may be kept longer. Advertising data collected by Google is retained under Google's policies.

We have no deletion request form, because we cannot link data to a person. To stop collection and cut the link to your data, uninstall the game or clear its data; stored analytics data then expires automatically as described above.

Data sent to Google by the ad and analytics SDKs travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). We run no servers of our own and keep no copy of this data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Game analytics data is linked only to a random app-instance ID, not to your identity, so we usually cannot find one person's data; uninstalling the game discards that ID, and a reinstall starts a new, unlinked one. Requests about advertising data are best directed to Google via the links above. For anything else, contact us at ryostu.game@gmail.com.

Ryo Games is the controller of game analytics data; Google acts as our processor. For advertising data, Google acts as an independent controller. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time under Privacy options. Google may process data on servers outside your country, including the United States. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Sale or sharing of personal information

Ryo Games does not sell personal information, and game analytics data is not shared for advertising. Google's use of data for advertising may be considered "sharing" under some US state laws; you can limit it with Privacy options where the game shows it, and by deleting your advertising ID in your device settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
