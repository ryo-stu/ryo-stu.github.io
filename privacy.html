Privacy Policy

Ryo Games · Effective date: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play games made by Ryo Games, including Stacker (Android). Ryo Games is an independent developer. If you have any question, contact us at ryostu.game@gmail.com.

Summary

  • We do not ask for, collect, or store any personal information ourselves. There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • Your best score and sound settings are saved only on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to serve and measure those ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps a small save file on your device containing your best score and your Music / SFX settings. It never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Stacker uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to offer an optional rewarded video ad ("Watch Ad" to continue a run). An ad is only shown when you choose to tap that button. To have an ad ready, the SDK starts when the game opens and loads ads in the background.

Google, as the ad provider, may collect and process the following to deliver, measure and protect ads (including fraud prevention):

  • device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app-set ID;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps, video completion) and diagnostics.

This data is collected and handled by Google under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Ryo Games does not receive this data in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated reports (for example, how many ads were shown).

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): on first launch the game shows Google's consent message, where you can consent, refuse, or manage your choices. If you refuse, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised.
  • Change your choice later: open the game's Settings (gear icon) and tap Privacy options. This option appears in regions where it is required.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Not watching ads: ads in Stacker are optional; you can always tap "No thanks".

Children

Stacker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through our game, contact us and we will help.

Sale or sharing of personal information

Ryo Games does not sell personal information. Google's use of data for advertising may be considered "sharing" under some US state laws; you can limit it through the consent options and device ad settings described above.

Data retention and security

We do not hold any of your data. Data on your device stays there until you uninstall the game. Data collected by Google is retained under Google's policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold personal data, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google via the links above. You are also welcome to contact us at ryostu.game@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
